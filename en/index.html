Measure → Diagnose → Prescribe → Log

  1. 01

    First, measure it

    Rhythm Training records the deviation of every hit in milliseconds. After a session you get Avg Deviation and Consistency, plus whether you tend to rush or drag.

  2. 02

    The Weakness Summary names the cause

    It reads your practice history and gives each module's weak spot a severity and a confidence. Because it is specific — confusing a minor 3rd with a major 3rd, say — there is no guessing about what to fix next.

  3. 03

    The Weekly Report decides what's next

    The diagnosis becomes a concrete menu for the week — down to the number of questions and measures — and it can be turned straight into a practice plan.

  4. 04

    Track the delta, not a feeling

    The completion screen compares score, Avg Deviation and Consistency against your previous session and logs a Personal Best. The judgment breakdown is kept hit by hit, so you can trace where it fell apart.
