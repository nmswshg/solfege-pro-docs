Support

Contact, restoring purchases, canceling your subscription, refunds, and system requirements

Contact

Bug reports, feature requests, and any other questions are all handled through the contact form below.

→ Open the contact form (Google Forms)

  • Response time: usually within 5 business days (for critical issues we aim to reply within 1 business day)
  • The same form is also reachable in the app via Settings → Support

Restore Purchases

After switching devices or reinstalling the app, use the "Restore Purchases" button at the bottom of the app's purchase screen. As long as you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the purchase, restoring is free.

Cancel Your Subscription

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone
  2. Tap your name
  3. Tap "Subscriptions"
  4. Select "Solfege PRO" and cancel

After canceling, you can keep using the subscription until the end of the current billing period.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple. Please submit your request at reportaproblem.apple.com. Whether a refund is granted is decided by Apple.

System Requirements

Solfege PRO runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 17.6 or later.
