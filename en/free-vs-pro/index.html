Foundation vs Pro

Compare the core practice that continues after 10 credits with the flexibility Pro unlocks.

What the Free Tier Gives You

The app is free to download, and you can try all nine trainings without entering a card. Your first 10 credits are shared across all trainings and let you try the same detailed settings as Pro.

The 10 credits do not expire and are not a time-limited trial. After they are used, every training remains available without a session limit using Foundation settings designed for core practice.

Choosing a Pro Plan

The Pro plans currently available are monthly and yearly subscriptions. Current regional prices are shown on the pricing page and on the purchase screen inside the app.

Purchase, cancellation, and restore all go through the App Store (your Apple ID). The steps are on the Support page.

Core Practice That Continues with Foundation

After the 10 credits are used, all nine trainings remain available without a session limit using Foundation settings. Pro unlocks control over advanced difficulty, item ranges, playback methods, question counts, weak-point modes, and more.
