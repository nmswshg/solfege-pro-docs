What You Can Do

Hear it, read it, lock the time, find it on your instrument, sing it, and keep the habit — the feature list, organised by what you want to achieve.

9 trainings

Solfege PRO is an iPhone and iPad practice app that puts 9 trainings and a practice-management layer in one place. This page lists them by what you will be able to do. For setting-by-setting detail, see the App Features Guide and the Manual.

Train Your Ear

Intervals, then chords, then scales, then progressions — so you can put a name to what you hear.

Interval Recognition

Hear the distance between two notes instantly — the foundation for figuring out music by ear.

13 intervals (unison to perfect octave) · ascending, descending, simultaneous and harmonic playback · a new root note each time · extra questions on the intervals you miss

→ Guide: building relative pitch

Chord Recognition

Name the chord you are hearing, by type.

5 difficulty tiers (basic triads → all triads → basic 7ths → all 7ths → extended) · block and arpeggiated playback · inversions · weak-point focus mode

→ Guide: building chord recognition

Scale Recognition

Go past major and minor: tell the seven modes apart by their sound.

From Beginner (major vs minor) up to all seven modes, step by step · focus on the characteristic tone that defines each mode · melodic-minor style option

→ Guide: hearing modal characteristic tones

Progression Training

Hear progressions as functions (T / SD / D) and the structure of a song starts to show.

3 answer formats (function T/SD/D, Roman numerals, chord symbols) · 2 to 4 chords per progression · Pop and Jazz categories · optional tonic preview before the question · accuracy per progression pattern

→ Guide: hearing functional harmony

Read Notation

Pitch and duration are split into two separate drills.

Sight Reading

The note name arrives the moment you see the note — you stop stalling on the reading.

Treble and bass clef · all 12 keys · accidentals on or off · ledger-line area on or off · response time recorded · error analysis that drills the notes you miss

→ Guide: the 5-landmark method

Rhythm Sight Reading

Play a rhythm you have never seen before, straight off the page.

24 curated patterns across 4 difficulty tiers, plus freshly generated notation each session · 4 strictness levels (Casual ±150 ms to Pro ±25 ms) · 40–180 BPM · rushing and dragging quantified

→ Settings manual

Lock In Your Timing

Turn "something feels off" into a number in milliseconds.

Rhythm Training

See whether you rush or drag, as an average error in milliseconds.

Two input modes — screen tap and microphone clap · 40–200 BPM · time signature, subdivision and click density · automatic calibration for your device's audio latency

→ Guide: rushing, dragging and the pocket

Metronome

The click your practice is measured against — no session limit, no purchase.

Always available from the Tools tab.

Find Notes on Your Instrument

Connect the note in your head to the place your finger goes.

Fretboard Training

Stop hunting for notes on the neck. Guitar and bass.

6 modes (note → position, position → note, string movement, ear → fret, interval → fret, melody echo) · standard, Drop D, Open G and other tunings · narrow the drill to specific strings and fret ranges · time-to-answer and weak-position statistics

→ Guide: octave shapes, range by range

Sing It Back

The step that closes the loop: produce the note you are hearing in your head, out loud.

Vocal Solfege

Sing from the staff and get pitch and rhythm scored on the spot, turning sight singing into practice you can do entirely in the app.

4 difficulty tiers (Beginner: C major, stepwise → Pro: every interval, every key) · listen to the model first · pitch guide (realtime, result only, or off) · pitch and rhythm scores plus a verdict per note · sharp / flat tendency read-out · a pitch-check screen to confirm the mic before you sing

Keep the Habit

Decide what to do, record it, and get told what is next.

Practice Plan

Bundle several trainings into one daily menu that shows only what is left today.

Per-item training settings are saved · remaining drills and estimated time · streak of consecutive practice days · a reminder at the time you choose · the free plan can hold up to 3 plans

Widgets & Control Center

Check progress and start in one tap, without opening the app.

Home Screen widget · three Lock Screen shapes (circular gauge, rectangular, inline) · a "Start Practice" control in Control Center (iOS 18+) · you add them yourself from the iOS widget gallery

Reports & Weakness Analysis

The app surfaces your weakest items and turns them into what to practise next.

Weekly report and practice calendar · accuracy per module and per item · a weekly recommended menu (free shows the top item, Pro shows all of them) · turn the recommendations straight into a practice plan

Screen Guides & Help

Shows which control to tap, one step at a time, on the real screen.

Screen-by-screen tutorials for 15 screens · replay any time from the Settings tab · a help (?) hint on individual settings · Japanese, English, French and German

Start Free

A shared free allowance of 10 sessions lets you try everything without entering a card. See the comparison table for what Pro adds.
