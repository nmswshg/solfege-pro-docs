Privacy Policy

What information Solfege PRO handles, and what it is used for.

Overview

Solfege PRO has no account registration. The app does not collect personal information such as your name or email address.

Information We Collect

Usage Data

To improve the app, we collect anonymous usage events via Google Firebase Analytics: which screens and trainings are used, practice statistics such as accuracy, plus device model, OS version, app version, and language. This data does not identify you personally.

Crash Reports

To fix defects, we collect crash reports via Firebase Crashlytics.

Microphone

The microphone is used only to detect claps and taps in rhythm trainings. Audio is processed on your device — it is never stored as a recording or sent to any server.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Apple. We never have access to your payment details, such as credit card numbers.

Contact Form

Anything you submit through the contact form (including any contact details you choose to provide) is used only to respond to your inquiry.

Data Deletion & Questions

For data-deletion requests or questions, please use the contact form below. Deleting the app erases the data stored on your device. Firebase identifiers are reset when the app is reinstalled.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase — see Privacy and Security in Firebase
  • Apple (App Store distribution and payment processing)

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page. Established: August 3, 2026.

Operator

Operated by the Solfege PRO team. Please reach us via the contact form above.
