Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the iOS and Android versions of “Solfege PRO” (the “App”) handle user information. The App has no account registration and does not ask you to enter your name or email address within the App.

2. Information We Handle

Usage Data

The App does not collect your name, email address, or similar information within the App. To improve the App and investigate issues, Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics may collect app usage, device, OS and app information, and crash information. This information is not used for advertising tracking. You can disable usage collection in the App's settings.

Crash Reports

To investigate and fix defects, we use Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports that may include stack traces, device, OS, and app information, and installation identifiers.

Microphone

The microphone is used only to detect claps and taps in rhythm trainings. Audio is processed on your device — it is never stored as a recording or sent to any server.

Contact Form

The App may open an external service (Google Forms) for inquiries and bug reports. Any information you enter in Google Forms is submitted and managed on that external service.

3. Purpose of Use

Information you voluntarily enter in the external form (for example, inquiry details, reproduction steps, and device or OS information), together with the usage and crash information described above, is used only for:

  • Responding to inquiries
  • Investigating and fixing issues
  • Improving the App

Providing contact information is generally not required. If you want a reply, you may choose to provide it.

4. Sharing with Third Parties

The App does not share collected information for third-party advertising. However, Google Firebase is used for usage analytics and crash diagnostics, and Google Forms is used for inquiries and bug reports; each service processes information as necessary. See Privacy and Security in Firebase for details.

5. External Links

The App and this website may contain links to external websites or services. Please review the privacy policies of those services for information about their data practices.

6. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as needed. The updated version will be posted on this page.

7. Contact

For inquiries and bug reports, please use the form linked on the Support page.
